Bulk Payment Upload

One row per student, with a column for each installment they paid. The sheet lists everyone who still owes something, along with their course fee, discount, paid so far and balance for reference. Receipt numbers are generated automatically.

Each row reads like: admission_no · name · class · course_fee · discount · paid_so_far · balance_due · installment_1 · date_1 · mode_1 · installment_2 · date_2 · mode_2 · …
Blank installments are skipped. The installments on a row cannot add up to more than that student's balance, and a payment identical to one already recorded is not added twice - so re-uploading the same sheet is safe.
