Student / Parent Login

Lets parents sign in at /portal with their WhatsApp number as both the username and the password, to see their own child's fees, attendance and class videos. Because a phone number is not a secret, anyone who knows a parent's number could sign in as them - the portal is therefore read-only. Leave this off until you want parents using it.
