Attendance
Teachers mark their own divisions; the office can mark any division from Attendance → Mark Attendance. Switching to twice a day keeps every register already taken, counted as a full day.
Turn this off if you would rather send one daily summary instead, so parents of absentees are not messaged twice.
Student / Parent Login

Lets parents sign in at /portal with their WhatsApp number as both the username and the password, to see their own child's fees, attendance and class videos. Because a phone number is not a secret, anyone who knows a parent's number could sign in as them - the portal is therefore read-only. Leave this off until you want parents using it.

Fee Payment Link (GPay / UPI)

Set the administrator's UPI ID here to enable "Pay via GPay/UPI" links in fee reminder WhatsApp messages sent to parents.
